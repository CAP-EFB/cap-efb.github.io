Mission Log

An offline mission log for Civil Air Patrol observers

The observer's kneeboard card on an iPad: checkpoint times, flight log, OPS checks, observations with photos, notes and sketch, the SAR results worksheet, and a signed PDF at the end. It works with no signal.

Install it on your iPad. Installed, it has its own app icon, opens full screen and keeps working with no connection. Using it as a web page in Safari is not enough.

Open Mission Log to install it
Open it in Safari on the iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The steps are below.

Everything you enter is stored on your device only. Nothing is shared with the cloud.

Install on an iPad

  1. Open Safari on the iPad and go to missionlog.io. It has to be Safari. Do this once while you have Wi-Fi or cellular.
  2. Tap the Share button. The square with an arrow pointing up, in the Safari toolbar.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add. You may need to scroll down in the list to find it. If you see an “Open as Web App” switch, leave it on.
  4. Close Safari and open Mission Log from its new Home Screen icon. Always start it from this icon. It opens full screen, like any other app.
  5. Read and accept the disclaimer, and allow Location if you want GPS positions. Location Services must be on in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Your data stays on your device

Mission Log has no account, no sign-in and no server. Logs, positions, photos, notes and signatures are stored locally on your iPad and are not sent to the cloud or to anyone else.

Data leaves the iPad only when you choose to export or share it yourself, for example the PDF or a backup file. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, keeping backups is up to you.

Good to know

  • Offline. After the first launch the app is stored on the iPad and works in the aircraft with no connection.
  • Back up after each mission. Use Menu → Export backup and save the PDF to Files. Removing the app from the Home Screen deletes its logs.
  • Updates are automatic. When the iPad is online, the app picks up a new version the next time you switch back to it.
  • GPS. Cellular iPads have a real GPS receiver. A Wi-Fi-only iPad needs an external GPS for a usable position in flight.
  • Feedback. Report issues, comments and ideas on the feedback form, also reachable from Menu → Send feedback in the app.

Before you fly with it

Mission Log is not for navigation and is not a certified electronic flight bag. Check times, positions and totals against the aircraft's instruments and official sources. It does not replace any form or report that Civil Air Patrol or your wing requires.

Try it on the ground first, and keep your paper log until you trust it.
